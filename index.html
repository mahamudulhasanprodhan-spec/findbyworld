Roadmap after the prototype

  1. Replace sample data with 100 real suppliers from export promotion agencies, trade-fair exhibitor lists and business registries.
  2. Test with founders on r/Entrepreneur, Indie Hackers and campus entrepreneurship groups.
  3. Add accounts and payments with a database such as Supabase and a payment provider such as Stripe.
  4. Launch "claim your listing" so suppliers verify and update their own profiles over email or WhatsApp.
